OPT-0080 — post-integration product correctness gate

This is a correctness, lifecycle, and responsiveness gate, not a timing or thermal comparison. It runs one fixed, one-way sequence with no automatic retry: forced depth-one control, forced depth-two candidate, ordinary production selection with no policy override, then ordinary seam-free cancellation. Every arm owns a fresh worker, backend, and WebGPU device.

The successful arms use the cache-only authenticated production packages and the canonical 96-second direct request. The page compares every final latent U32, every raw waveform U32, the full C2378 stitch neighborhood, and every normalized WAV byte. Large Blobs are compared in bounded blocks and released from the page before cancellation.

Supply the frozen run identity in the URL query: coreCommit, harnessCommit, machineModel, osVersion, osBuild, browserVersion, gpuCoreCount, and memoryBytes.
